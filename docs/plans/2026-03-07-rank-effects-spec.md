# Rank Effects Spec

## Objective

Replace the current generic "anime badge" look with a tiered visual system that feels authored, readable at small size, and emotionally aligned with the ranking ladder. The goal is not literal franchise mimicry. The goal is to evoke the same energy grammar: heat, power-up, cosmos, charge, menace, and collapse.

## Core Principles

- The badge must read clearly at 48 px first and scale up cleanly.
- Every tier needs one dominant silhouette and one dominant motion pattern.
- Energy must have value falloff: bright core, softer mid-glow, fading outer aura.
- Motion must communicate character, not randomness.
- The Diamond tier is the reference quality bar for all later tiers.

## Shared Structure

Each rank effect uses four layers:

1. Core badge shape: the readable rank medallion with score text.
2. Inner energy: tight glow hugging the badge edge.
3. Outer aura: tier-specific light shape with soft opacity falloff.
4. Ambient motion: particles, rays, rings, sparks, or embers.

## Per-Rank Visual Direction

### Diamond

- Theme: god-tier cosmic charge.
- Color: white-hot center, cyan edge, deep indigo shadow.
- Shape language: faceted diamond core with long tapered energy rays.
- Motion: slow celestial rotation plus intermittent surge pulses.
- Emission: layered radial and linear gradients, thin lens-flare streaks, and airy plasma wisps.

### Ruby

- Theme: violent heat and blood-red pressure.
- Color: rose core, crimson flame, dark wine shadow.
- Shape language: sharp crown spikes and pressure waves.
- Motion: aggressive pulse with short forward bursts.
- Emission: hot inner bloom and jagged flame petals.

### Sapphire

- Theme: stable cold power.
- Color: electric blue, ice edge, navy shadow.
- Shape language: clean shards and orbiting arcs.
- Motion: steady orbit with occasional bright flash.
- Emission: crystalline ribbons rather than flames.

### Amethyst

- Theme: occult resonance.
- Color: violet core, lilac fringe, midnight shadow.
- Shape language: hex rings and rune-like spokes.
- Motion: trance-like rotation and echo ripples.
- Emission: smoky translucent gradients with soft interference rings.

### Emerald

- Theme: living force.
- Color: bright jade, mint edge, forest shadow.
- Shape language: circular seal with leaf-like energy slivers.
- Motion: breathing expansion and controlled ripple.
- Emission: smooth aura shell with rising motes.

### Gold

- Theme: medal plus solar charge.
- Color: warm gold, pale yellow highlight, bronze shadow.
- Shape language: round badge with sunburst spokes.
- Motion: measured pulse and shimmer sweep.
- Emission: gilded halo and restrained sparkles.

### Topaz

- Theme: narrow situational spark.
- Color: amber, honey, dusty brown.
- Shape language: square seal with angled warning cuts.
- Motion: slow flicker and intermittent edge sweep.
- Emission: thin electric seams, not full aura bloom.

### Silver

- Theme: low heat, metallic drift.
- Color: cool gray, pale white, steel blue.
- Shape language: circular plate and faint spoke mesh.
- Motion: low-amplitude shimmer only.
- Emission: reflective glints, minimal aura mass.

### Aquamarine

- Theme: weak current.
- Color: aqua, pale cyan, dark teal.
- Shape language: shield with waterline rings.
- Motion: gentle ripple and faint current drift.
- Emission: translucent wave contours instead of sparks.

### Jade

- Theme: dull static life.
- Color: moss, gray-green, dark olive.
- Shape language: shield or pill with restrained perimeter haze.
- Motion: barely-there pulse.
- Emission: thin fog halo only.

### Garnet

- Theme: hostile pressure.
- Color: rust red, scarlet edge, near-black shadow.
- Shape language: cracked shield and broken burst fragments.
- Motion: unstable twitch and collapse pulse.
- Emission: short-lived spark spits, no majestic aura.

### Onyx

- Theme: dead zone.
- Color: charcoal, gunmetal, ashy highlight.
- Shape language: heavy slab, no ornament.
- Motion: almost none.
- Emission: weak murky shadow, intentionally lifeless.

### Bottom Tier

- Theme: failure / void.
- Color: near-black with sickly low-value tint.
- Shape language: flat pill or scarred plate.
- Motion: none.
- Emission: absent by design.

## Diamond Reference Requirements

The Diamond prototype is successful only if it has all of the following:

- A clearly readable faceted core at 48 px.
- A visible bright-to-soft gradient in the emitted aura.
- Motion that feels intentional rather than noisy.
- Distinct inner, middle, and outer light zones.
- Better small-size readability than the current inline SVG badge.
